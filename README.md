# Fish Evolution

A browser and iPad fish-eating game. Eat smaller fish, evolve through five stages, avoid larger fish and bosses, and collect occasional golden lightning pickups for a temporary speed boost.

## Play

https://datazoe.github.io/fish-evolution-game/

Use WASD or arrow keys on desktop, or the on-screen touch controls or touch-and-drag on iPad. Colliding with a fish that is too large to eat costs evolution energy. Boss fish appear periodically, have a health bar, and award bonus score and energy when defeated. A lightning pickup spawns every 18–30 seconds near the current view and grants a 6.5-second speed boost. Press **Rebirth** to confirm and restart the run.

If the page looks outdated after a deployment, hard-refresh it (Ctrl+F5 on Windows, or clear the browser cache) after the GitHub Pages deployment completes.
